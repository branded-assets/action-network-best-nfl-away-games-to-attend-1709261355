Key Takeaways

The Headlines From 239 Road Trips

  • Giants @ Eagles, Nov. 8, is the best trip in the NFL. A 83.8 Travel Index, the highest of any game scored, no flight required, and a two-hour drive.
  • Buffalo's trip to Miami on Jan. 3 is the worst deal in football. Dead last of 239 games on cost: a $503.79 ticket, $322.37 hotel, and $611.99 in travel costs for a 1,187-mile flight.
  • Arizona has the two cheapest trips in the league. Both to SoFi Stadium, with the Sept. 13 Chargers game topping the Cost Index at a $193.73 average ticket.
  • Commanders @ Cowboys is the fiercest rivalry on the board. A perfect 100 rivalry score, the only game in the dataset to hit the max.
  • Arizona also has the best weather in the NFL, four times over. Four of the top five weather scores in the entire dataset belong to home games at State Farm Stadium.
